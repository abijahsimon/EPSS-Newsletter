## The Programme

Commencement and Awards Celebration took place at 12:30 p.m. on Sunday, June 14, 2026. The program included graduation formalities and a welcome lunch, followed by the presentation of degree candidates and undergraduate awards.

- Opening remarks: Professor Jonathan Aurnou, EPSS Chair
- Special guest speaker: Professor Laurie Leshin, University Professor for Space Futures, Arizona State University
- Remarks by the salutatorian: Sara Kang
- Graduate degree candidates: Professor Bjorn Benneke, Graduate Advisor
- Undergraduate awards: Professor Mackenzie Day, Associate Professor
- Baccalaureate degree candidates: Professor Mackenzie Day, Associate Professor
- Closing remarks: Professor Jonathan Aurnou
