Commencement · Sunday, June 14, 2026

Celebrating the Class of 2026

Sixty-five degrees conferred, thirteen summer field scholarships, and a group photograph on the steps of the Geology building.

The Programme

Commencement and Awards Celebration

12:30 p.m., Sunday 14 June 2026. Graduation formalities and welcome lunch, followed by the presentation of degree candidates and undergraduate awards.

  1. Opening remarks Professor Jonathan Aurnou, EPSS Chair
  2. Special guest speaker Professor Laurie Leshin, University Professor for Space Futures, Arizona State University
  3. Remarks by the salutatorian Sara Kang
  4. Graduate degree candidates Professor Björn Benneke, Graduate Advisor
  5. Undergraduate awards Professor Mackenzie Day, Associate Professor
  6. Baccalaureate degree candidates Professor Mackenzie Day, Associate Professor
  7. Closing remarks Professor Jonathan Aurnou
