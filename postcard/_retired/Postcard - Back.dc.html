What’s changed

The newsletter is now digital.

For decades this arrived as a printed booklet. Beginning with the 2025–2026 issue, it lives online — no page limit, photographs in full color, and room for far more of our students, alumni, and faculty than paper ever allowed.

What hasn’t changed: it’s still annual, still edited here in the department, still the newsletter you’ve read for years.
